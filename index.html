How to type it — exactly as the paper reads:
SECTION - A (READING) a section heading — write SECTION A 6 marks and the marks are printed at the right of the heading · Q1. a question · A. a lettered part · a. a sub-part · i. one  ii. two  iii. three options on one line · marks at the end of the line: (1), (3x1=3), (4x0.5=2) or [2]
BOX: baskets | shopkeeper | money a help box · MATCH: MASCULINE | FEMININE then uncle | niece on each following line · TABLE: Reason | Percentage then Lower prices | 32% on each following line, a ruled table · FRAME: the lines below it, up to END FRAME, inside a box (FRAME: 30 alone, an empty 30 mm box) · LINES: 4 four ruled answer lines · PIC: a picture (write PIC: 50 for a taller one; a PIC: straight after a BOX: prints beside it) · ____ a blank to fill · a blank line leaves a gap.

To change the weight of a few words only: *bold* prints in bold, ~plain~ prints without bold on a line that is otherwise bold.

Only needed when a question has a picture (type PIC: on its own line where it should print). JPG or PNG files; or copy a picture / take a screenshot and press Ctrl+V anywhere outside the typing boxes.

3  Formatting

Examples, one per line or joined with "and" or commas: font size 13 · bigger font · font Times New Roman · line spacing roomy · more space between lines · no indent · questions not bold · hide name line · Q3 marks 2 · Q5 marks 4x1=4 · add 4 lines after Q5 · remove lines from Q2 · add a gap after Q6 · delete Q7 · renumber · replace colour with color · make "not" bold · title Unit Test 1 · max marks 40 · centre the word "or" · align questions left · fix cut-off text · format the paper properly · fix errors · check · pdf · undo. On a Word paper kept in its own layout, the change is made there too, so its boxes and pictures stay.

4  Check & print

Save the PDF on this computer: the window opens — set Destination to Save as PDF, press Save and choose the folder. The file arrives named after the paper itself, Class-II-English-Appraisal-Worksheet-2.pdf, not "index.html", so it is easy to find again. Keep the paper size at A4 and Headers and footers off.
Print copies sends the same paper to the printer instead.

The checker catches mechanical and structural mistakes — spacing, punctuation, numbering, marks that do not add up, repeated questions, and any word that is not in the British-English dictionary (names, which begin with a capital, are left alone). It is not a substitute for reading the paper: it cannot tell you that an answer is wrong or a question is unfair.
