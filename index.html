How to type it — exactly as the paper reads:
SECTION - A (READING) a section heading · Q1. a question · A. a lettered part · a. a sub-part · i. one  ii. two  iii. three options on one line · marks at the end of the line: (1), (3x1=3), (4x0.5=2) or [2]
BOX: baskets | shopkeeper | money a help box · MATCH: MASCULINE | FEMININE then uncle | niece on each following line · LINES: 4 four ruled answer lines · PIC: a picture (write PIC: 50 for a taller one; a PIC: straight after a BOX: prints beside it) · ____ a blank to fill · a blank line leaves a gap.

To change the weight of a few words only: *bold* prints in bold, ~plain~ prints without bold on a line that is otherwise bold.

4  Check & print

Save the PDF on this computer: the window opens — set Destination to Save as PDF, press Save and choose the folder. The file arrives named after the paper itself, Class-II-English-Appraisal-Worksheet-2.pdf, not "index.html", so it is easy to find again. Keep the paper size at A4 and Headers and footers off.
Print copies sends the same paper to the printer instead.

The checker catches mechanical and structural mistakes — spacing, punctuation, numbering, marks that do not add up, repeated questions, and any word that is not in the British-English dictionary (names, which begin with a capital, are left alone). It is not a substitute for reading the paper: it cannot tell you that an answer is wrong or a question is unfair.
